# Fathom

Down here, your light is all you have. Slip into a flooded maze as a tiny glowing
forager, grazing constellations of plankton in water that swallows everything
beyond your reach. In the Kindle dive, each meal opens your circular window a
little wider. Stop feeding and the dark closes in again.

An echo can find what your eyes cannot. Send a cyan pulse winding around corners
to uncover passages and catch a glimpse of the hunters inside them. The maze
remembers every place you have touched, but familiar ground disappears beyond
your window. Finding your way means trusting both your memory and your next ping.

You are not the only thing listening. Lanternjaw follows your glow, hiding its
jaws beneath the same amber light as a harmless drifter. Gloamfin sends violet
echoes through the corridors and races toward the sound you leave behind.
Flarefish gives one warm warning before its bloom burns through the rock. Ink
can break a hunter's sight; it cannot silence you.

Eat the final mote and the trench opens beneath you. Your score carries deeper,
the hunters grow more numerous, and your sonar reaches a little less far. Steal
an amber meal, take the next corner, and keep one light ahead of the abyss.
